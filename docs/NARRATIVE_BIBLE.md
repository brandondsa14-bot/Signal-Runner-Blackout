# Signal Runner: Blackout — Narrative Bible

## Core mystery

Signal Runner: Blackout takes place outside the fictional town of Marshall, Nebraska.

The public believes St. Mercy Cemetery is abandoned. Beneath it is a decommissioned civil-defense communications annex later repurposed by Dr. Adrian Vale for **Project Resonance**, a covert neural-response experiment built around three radio-frequency carriers.

The cemetery was selected because its old service tunnels gave the annex concealed access and unusually isolated underground space for radio-frequency testing.

## The protagonist

The player is the Marshall police officer already assigned to the cluster of four disappearances.

The officer is contacted because the fourth missing person, Leah Morrison, had previously spoken to the investigation and knew which officer was handling the linked cases.

## The four missing people

### Lena Park
A local reporter investigating unexplained activity around the supposedly abandoned cemetery.

### Mateo Ruiz
An electrical contractor who had previously serviced a buried utility relay and recognized that power was still being drawn beneath the cemetery.

### Evan Cole
A cemetery groundskeeper who discovered evidence that the old service tunnels had been reopened.

### Leah Morrison
An EMT and friend of Lena. Leah helped the group document the site after Lena connected the other three leads.

The four met at the cemetery gate to document the reopened annex. Vale's staff intercepted and detained them.

The damaged group photograph in Storage shows them together at the gate, confirming that the disappearances were connected rather than coincidental.

## Who made the phone call?

Leah Morrison.

After surviving transfer to an Observation room, Leah recovered access to a maintenance radio. At 2:14 AM she reached the Marshall PD repeater and contacted the officer assigned to the disappearances.

The opening deliberately hides her identity. The ending confirms it after the evidence upload.

## The underground facility

The structure began as a civil-defense signal annex. Dr. Adrian Vale later acquired access through a shell research program and converted it into the Project Resonance laboratory.

The original communications infrastructure explains why the facility contains:

- relay rooms;
- receiver calibration equipment;
- shielded archives;
- security frequency routers;
- multiple signal channels;
- the evidence mirror;
- buried access under an otherwise low-traffic site.

## Project Resonance

Project Resonance attempted to use a conductive neural matrix and controlled RF carrier frequencies to influence and stabilize human neural activity.

The scanner carried by the player descends from the same maintenance hardware used by facility security and technicians. Its RED, BLUE and YELLOW carriers are therefore useful for:

- tuning relays;
- bypassing frequency locks;
- exposing treated/erased markings;
- aligning receivers;
- operating containment systems.

This makes the scanner part of the story world rather than an arbitrary puzzle device.

## Subject C-17 / the Clay Stalker

**C-17 is Elias Voss**, formerly a facility security officer.

Voss was injured during an early Project Resonance incident. Vale authorized emergency use of the prototype conductive neural matrix. Repeated carrier exposure caused progressive neurological failure and uncontrolled tissue/matrix growth.

The result is the Clay Stalker.

C-17 still reacts to the same carrier frequencies used by the facility, which explains why scanner pulses and noisy interactions can attract it.

## Dr. Adrian Vale

Vale is the director responsible for reopening the annex, continuing Project Resonance, detaining the four civilians and suppressing evidence.

Earlier documents foreshadow this:

- Reception establishes that the annex is still being operated.
- Archive shows Vale adding civilian names without notifying police.
- Laboratory identifies the C-17 carrier response.
- Detention proves civilians were held rather than merely missing.
- Maintenance explains how the facility's three signals support Containment.
- Final Investigation contains Vale's purge order.

## Story progression

### Beginning
The officer receives the anonymous 2:14 AM call, reaches the cemetery visitor gate, obtains access and uncovers the hidden stairway.

### Investigation
Reception, Security and Archive establish that the underground site is active and that all four missing people passed through it.

### Rising danger
The Laboratory connects Project Resonance to C-17. Detention and Maintenance prove the civilians were held and reveal the scale of the facility.

### Climax
Containment confirms that the Clay Stalker is Elias Voss and releases the critical C-17 archive.

### Resolution
The officer decrypts the evidence mirror and transmits the case to Marshall PD.

The upload identifies Vale, preserves the Project Resonance evidence and gives investigators the location before the site can be purged.

## Ending

Three victims are recovered dead. Leah Morrison is recovered alive from Observation.

The evidence identifies Elias Voss as C-17 and links Vale directly to the abductions and cover-up.

The 2:14 AM call is confirmed to have come from Leah.

Vale is arrested before dawn and the annex is seized and sealed by investigators.

## Foreshadowing rules

Narrative clues should:

1. Answer or complicate an existing investigation question.
2. Be short enough to read during play.
3. Become more meaningful when later evidence is found.
4. Never contradict the objective/puzzle dependencies.
5. Avoid explaining C-17 completely before Containment.
